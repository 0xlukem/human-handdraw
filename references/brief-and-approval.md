# Brief and approval gate

Collect only missing decisions. Never make the user repeat information already established.

## Required brief

- Visible-copy language: Spanish or English. Always ask this explicitly.
- Message: exact copy or a topic from which copy may be proposed.
- Context: what the underlying video shows and where the overlay must leave visual room.
- Canvas: aspect ratio and pixel dimensions. Offer 1080 × 1920 at 30 fps for vertical social video.
- Duration: ask every time. Estimate from the amount of writing and reserve 20–30% of the duration for the completed composition to remain readable.
- Palette: base stroke color and no more than one functional accent unless the reference clearly requires more.
- Emphasis: words or relationships that deserve a rough circle, repeated underline, strike, arrow, burst, or basic icon.
- Optional input: reference image, brand constraints, or b-roll for a compositing test.

If a topic contains factual claims, verify them with authoritative primary sources before proposing copy. Keep citations in the plan or notes, not in the visible overlay unless the user asks.

## Approval packet

Present all five items together:

1. Final proposed copy and language.
2. A simple static composition sketch showing relative placement and safe space.
3. The reveal order, including what each connection means.
4. A timing sheet with writing, drawing, emphasis, and final reading hold.
5. The planned hand-drawn devices and colors.

State which details remain assumptions. Ask for explicit approval of this packet. Creation begins only after that approval.

## Review loop

After creation, render the opaque preview and show it before the transparent master. Discuss legibility, rhythm, hierarchy, spacing, and whether the hand feels controlled rather than synthetic. Revise the source and preview until the user approves it.
